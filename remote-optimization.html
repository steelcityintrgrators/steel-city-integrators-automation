REMOTE SESSION WORKFLOW

Controlled access, not an open back door.

GatherBeam does not expose church networks directly to the public internet and does not ask customers to email credentials.

01 · Qualify

Remote Ready

Verify network reliability, supported devices, administrative permissions, backup capability, and secure access path.

  • Equipment compatibility
  • Network review
  • Access method
02 · Authorize

Defined Scope

The church approves who may connect, which systems may be touched, and what work is authorized.

  • Ticket/session ID
  • Approved devices
  • Access window
03 · Adjust

Technician Session

The technician captures pre-change state, connects securely, diagnoses, changes only approved settings, and verifies live behavior.

  • Pre-change snapshot
  • Authorized adjustments
  • Live verification
04 · Document

Close & Learn

Every session ends with a change log, customer summary, rollback information, and structured data for future support.

  • Change history
  • Result summary
  • Knowledge capture
SUPPORTED WORK

What a remote technician may be able to adjust.

Final scope depends on equipment support and authorization.

Audio

Console & DSP

Gain structure, EQ, dynamics, routing, matrices, broadcast mix, scene/show files, processor settings.

Video

PTZ & Switching

Exposure, white balance, presets, color matching, supported switching and camera-control settings.

Streaming

OBS / vMix / Encoders

Bitrate, resolution, frame rate, routing, output settings, profiles, supported software and appliances.

Network

Managed Devices

Documented switches, VLANs, device addressing and other approved network changes when within technician scope.

Workflow

Operator Setup

Scenes, presets, repeatable startup/shutdown, volunteer handoffs and documented Sunday workflows.

Escalation

Onsite When Necessary

Physical cabling, replacement, mounting, electrical coordination, rigging and inaccessible systems move to GatherBeam Onsite.

Remote-first church technology operations.
