WHAT GATHERBEAM WILL OFFER

Start with the need.
Grow into the whole system.

GatherBeam combines remote-first technical help with tools for streaming, church communication, visitor follow-up, and growth. We are building and validating each branch separately.

Founding pilot

Livestream & AV Health Check

A $149 remote review of one recent service, current equipment/settings, and your biggest recurring issue. Receive prioritized findings across audio, video, streaming, network, graphics, and workflow.

Starts with the system you already have. Equipment, guaranteed fixes, and onsite work are outside the pilot scope.

BETA

GatherBeam LeadFlow

Church discovery, local opportunity mapping, evidence-based livestream reviews, and a clear path from a need to the right next step.

A watchlist is not a diagnosis. Findings require direct review and evidence.

BETA

GatherBeam CRM

Church relationship tools for contacts, inquiries, visitor interest, consented follow-up, service history, and reporting.

Each church’s visitor records stay private to that church workspace.

BETA

GatherBeam BeamBox

A church-oriented streaming device and setup designed around a validated production workflow.

Supported equipment, setup, pricing, and support will be published after a working pilot is validated.

BETA

GatherBeam Stream

Branded streaming software for smart TVs, phones, and apps, including a church channel experience for live and on-demand services.

Platform compatibility will be listed only after end-to-end testing.

BETA

GatherBeam Growth

Marketing and growth management for churches: goals, content, campaigns, visitor journeys, approved integrations, and outcome insights.

Churches approve content and account activity. Live publishing and messaging require authorization.

Beta policy: every branch remains marked BETA until its core workflow is built, connected, and validated. Concepts, mockups, and plans do not count as completed products.
