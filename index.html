WHAT GATHERBEAM WILL OFFER

Start with the need.
Grow into the whole system.

GatherBeam is the umbrella for GatherBeam Ministry, GatherBeam Church AI, streaming hardware and software, Roku, and GatherBeam’s Android and Apple apps. Lead generation and CRM work behind the scenes as shared backend systems.

Founding pilot

Livestream & AV Health Check

A $149 remote review of one recent service, current equipment/settings, and your biggest recurring issue. Receive prioritized findings across audio, video, streaming, network, graphics, and workflow.

Starts with the system you already have. Equipment, guaranteed fixes, and onsite work are outside the pilot scope.

GatherBeam BeamBox

A church streaming device that helps route a church’s existing stream to GatherBeam viewing destinations, including Roku and GatherBeam’s Smart TV, Android, and Apple apps.

Designed to connect a church’s existing streaming workflow with GatherBeam’s Smart TV and Android/Apple app experience. Device compatibility is being validated.

BETA

GatherBeam Streaming Software

Branded streaming software that makes a church’s live and on-demand services available as an additional destination through GatherBeam’s Roku channel and Smart TV, Android, and Apple apps.

An additional viewing destination alongside a church’s existing platforms. Each platform and playback path must be tested before release.

BETA

GatherBeam Ministry & GatherBeam Church AI

Church-focused AI and connected software for ministry workflows, visitor experiences, communications, and insights, organized under the GatherBeam umbrella.

GatherBeam Ministry and Church AI are product systems within GatherBeam. Capabilities remain beta until each workflow is connected and validated.
