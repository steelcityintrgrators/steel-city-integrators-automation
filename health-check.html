What we review

Audio, video, stream and workflow—together.

A livestream problem often has multiple causes. The report focuses on evidence and prioritized fixes rather than generic best practices.

Audio

Gain structure, speech intelligibility, EQ, compression, broadcast mix strategy, routing and obvious signal-flow problems.

Video

Exposure, white balance, color matching, PTZ framing, camera consistency, switching and lighting interaction.

Stream & network

Resolution, frame rate, bitrate, encoder/software settings, upstream capacity, packet loss and repeatable reliability issues.

Operator workflow

Volunteer complexity, scene/preset consistency, handoffs, checklists and failure points that technology alone will not solve.
