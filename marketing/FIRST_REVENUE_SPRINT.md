# Steel City Integrators — First Revenue Sprint

Date: 2026-10-21

## Objective
Convert the existing operating system and prospect database into the first new external paid customer as fast as practical, without adding unnecessary product features.

## Strategic rule
No additional website redesign, app development, broad automation expansion, new branding work, or speculative feature work until:
1. at least 20 qualified prospects have been personally contacted,
2. at least 5 real discovery conversations have occurred, and
3. at least 1 external prospect has been asked to buy a defined paid service.

## Primary offer ladder
Use the already-published service menu:
- Phone support — starting at $40 / 30 minutes
- Text support — starting at $40 / 30 minutes
- Remote technical support — starting at $150/hour
- Remote consultation — starting at $350
- On-site consultation / assessment — starting at $500
- Installation / project work — custom proposal

For commercial organizations, prioritize $350 remote consultations, $500+ on-site assessments, and installation proposals over $40 support unless the prospect has a narrow troubleshooting need.

## Minimum Revenue Path
1. Work the prioritized outreach queue.
2. Review each prospect's public source before contact.
3. Ronald makes or owns the first human contact for the top accounts.
4. Lead with a specific business outcome, not an equipment pitch.
5. Ask one qualifying question.
6. Offer a paid assessment when a credible need exists.
7. Put qualified responses into the existing CRM.
8. Schedule using the existing booking workflow.
9. If payment remains disabled, issue a manual invoice/payment link only through an approved processor and do not represent checkout as live.
10. Convert the assessment into a project proposal or support retainer.

## Ten-business-day target
- 30 personalized contacts
- 10 live conversations or substantive email replies
- 5 qualified discovery calls
- 3 paid-assessment asks
- 1–3 paid assessments
- 1 installation proposal
- 1 recurring-support proposal

These are operating targets, not guaranteed conversion rates.

## Church outreach positioning
Do not imply the church has a broken system merely because it livestreams.

Opening:
"Hi, this is Ronald Williams with Steel City Integrators in Birmingham. I work with churches on sound, livestreaming, PTZ cameras, networking, confidence monitors and the systems volunteers have to operate every week. I saw that your church actively streams services. I’m reaching out to see whether there is anything in the current workflow you would like to make more reliable or easier for the team to run."

Qualifying questions:
- What is the one technical issue your team spends the most time dealing with?
- Are volunteers or staff operating the system?
- Is the larger priority sound in the room, the online stream, cameras, networking, or overall workflow?
- Is there an upgrade already being considered in the next 3–12 months?

Close:
"If it would help, I can do a structured remote review for $350 or an on-site assessment starting at $500. The goal is to give you a practical action plan even if you do not buy equipment from us."

## Commercial/trades outreach positioning
Opening:
"Hi, this is Ronald Williams with Steel City Integrators in Birmingham. We help commercial organizations with structured cabling, Wi-Fi/network infrastructure, CCTV, access control and commercial displays. I’m reaching out because your operation appears to have the kind of office/field or multi-location environment where infrastructure reliability and visibility matter. Is there any network, camera, access or cabling work already on your radar?"

Qualifying questions:
- Are you planning a move, expansion, renovation, or additional location?
- Are there dead zones, cabling limitations, camera gaps, access-control needs, or display/dispatch requirements?
- Who owns facilities/IT decisions?
- Is there a project budget or deadline already established?

Close:
"We can start with an on-site assessment from $500 and then quote only the work that is justified."

## Follow-up cadence
For a manually reviewed business contact:
- Day 0: personalized email or call
- Day 2–3: one short follow-up
- Day 7: final value-first follow-up
- Then stop active follow-up unless the prospect engages

Honor opt-outs and suppression immediately. Do not automate cold SMS. Use public business contact paths and appropriate business hours.

## Metrics that matter
Track:
- prospect contacts
- conversations
- qualified opportunities
- paid-assessment asks
- paid assessments
- proposals
- wins
- collected revenue
- recurring monthly revenue
- lost reason

Do not optimize on page views, generic social impressions, or feature count before these conversion metrics exist.
