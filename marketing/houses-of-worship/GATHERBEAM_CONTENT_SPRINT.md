## Day 1 — Reel: the room mix is not the stream mix

**Length:** 20 seconds  
**On-screen hook:** “Your sanctuary can sound great while the livestream sounds different.”

**Voiceover:** “The room and the livestream are two different listening environments. If online speech gets buried when music starts, check which console output feeds the stream and whether it has its own balance. Trace the signal before shopping for another mixer.”

**Faceless visuals:** abstract console meter animation; simple Room Mix → Broadcast Bus → Stream diagram; headphone waveform. No church footage or customer claims.

**Caption:** A strong house mix does not automatically create a clear broadcast mix. Start by identifying the exact output feeding the encoder, then listen to a short recording from that output. GatherBeam helps churches work from evidence before buying gear.

**CTA:** “Need a second set of eyes? Request the GatherBeam Church Livestream & AV Health Check.”

## Day 3 — Carousel: five checks before replacing a camera

Slides:
1. “Before buying a new church PTZ camera, check these five things.”
2. “White balance: are cameras set to the same color temperature?”
3. “Exposure: do faces stay consistent when stage lighting changes?”
4. “Preset framing: do volunteer-friendly positions exist and repeat?”
5. “Lighting: is the subject lit evenly, or is the camera fighting the room?”
6. “Switching: can operators see previews and make confident cuts?”
7. “If the image still fails, document the camera model, settings and a sample clip.”

**Caption:** Camera symptoms can come from settings, lighting, framing or the switching workflow. Identify the constraint before replacing hardware.

**CTA:** “GatherBeam’s $149 founding-pilot Health Check reviews submitted service evidence and equipment details.”

## Day 4 — Reel: three checks for muddy speech

**Length:** 25 seconds  
**On-screen hook:** “Pastor sounds muddy online? Check the path first.”

**Voiceover:** “One: confirm the stream receives the intended microphone and console bus. Two: listen to a local recording from the stream output. Three: compare levels at each handoff so one device is not clipping while another is too quiet. Change one thing at a time and record again.”

**Faceless visuals:** signal-flow line; labeled meter graphic; A/B waveform. Avoid showing unsafe cabling or unverified customer systems.

**Caption:** Muddy speech can have several causes. These checks help locate where the sound changes; they do not assume the church needs new equipment.

**CTA:** “Get a prioritized remote review through GatherBeam.”

## Day 5 — Facebook text post: Sunday preflight

“Before Sunday, record a short test from the same output that feeds your livestream. Check that speech is clear, music is balanced, meters are not clipping, the correct camera presets load, and someone can hear the online mix. Save the current console scene before making changes. What part of the preflight is hardest for your team?”

## Day 6 — Story sequence: volunteer workflow

1. “A reliable Sunday setup should not depend on one person remembering every step.”
2. “Label repeatable scenes and camera presets clearly.”
3. “Keep a short startup and recovery checklist near the control position.”
4. Question sticker: “What step does your team still have to remember from scratch?”

Reply only with practical, relevant advice. Do not diagnose a church publicly from a comment.

**On-screen hook:** “Your church may not need a new mixer.”

**Voiceover:** “If the room sounds good but the stream does not, first check the broadcast output, monitoring, gain structure and volunteer workflow. A new console cannot fix a signal path nobody has traced. Start with the system you already have.”

**Faceless visuals:** “Problem → Trace → Test → Decide” motion graphic; clean GatherBeam wordmark; CTA card.

**Caption:** Better troubleshooting starts with evidence. GatherBeam reviews a recent service and the submitted system details, then returns prioritized findings, practical tests and a remote-versus-onsite recommendation.

**CTA:** “Request the $149 founding-pilot Church Livestream & AV Health Check.”
