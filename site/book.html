Booking protection

Simple rules for a reserved appointment.

  • Payment: Full service fee is due before the appointment is confirmed.
  • Non-refundable: Fees reserve professional time and are non-refundable, except if Steel City Integrators cancels or law requires otherwise.
  • Rescheduling: One reschedule is allowed with at least 24 hours’ notice.
  • Late or missed session: The session ends at the original end time; missed appointments forfeit the fee.
  • Scope: Each booking covers one primary issue or project need within the listed time.
Common questions

Know what happens before you pay.

Is the consultation fee applied to a future project?

For an installation agreement of $1,000 or more signed within 30 days, Steel City Integrators may credit up to $99 of the consultation fee toward labor. Travel add-ons and completed troubleshooting time are not credited.

What if the problem cannot be fixed in one session?

You still receive the reserved diagnostic time and a recommended next step. Parts, equipment, additional labor or a return visit require approval and are billed separately.

How is on-site distance calculated?

Travel zones use normal road mileage from central Birmingham to the service address. Locations beyond approximately 80 miles require a custom travel quote.

Can Steel City support systems it did not install?

Yes. Support depends on safe access, equipment condition, available documentation and compatibility. A consultation does not guarantee repair or manufacturer support.
