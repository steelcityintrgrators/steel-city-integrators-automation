Booking policy

  • Prepayment required. No appointment is confirmed without successful payment.
  • Non-refundable reservation. Fees are non-refundable once the appointment is booked or the service window is reserved, except when Steel City Integrators cancels or applicable law requires otherwise.
  • One reschedule. One change is allowed with at least 24 hours’ notice, subject to availability.
  • Late and missed sessions. Sessions end at the original end time. Missed appointments forfeit the reservation fee, subject to applicable law.
  • Safe access required. Remote control is temporary and customer-authorized. Unsafe, unlawful or unsupported work may be declined.

By paying, you agree to the service terms.

Before you book

Common questions.

What if the issue cannot be fixed in one session?

You still receive the reserved diagnostic time and a recommended next step. Parts, equipment, additional labor or a return visit require approval and are billed separately.

Can you support systems you did not install?

Yes, when the system can be accessed safely and the equipment, documentation and manufacturer restrictions allow it. A consultation does not guarantee repair.

How is on-site mileage calculated?

The zone is based on normal road mileage from central Birmingham to the service address. If the wrong zone is selected, the difference must be paid before the visit is confirmed.

How are full installations scheduled?

Start with a paid on-site assessment or project request. After the scope is approved, the installation date is secured by the deposit stated in the proposal.
