Booking & Service Terms

Effective: October 20, 2026

These terms apply to online consultations, technical-support sessions and on-site assessment or troubleshooting reservations with Steel City Integrators LLC. A separate written proposal or agreement may govern installation projects and controls if its terms conflict with these terms.

Payment and appointment confirmation

Full payment of the listed reservation fee is required before an appointment time can be selected. A charge alone does not create an appointment until an available time is selected and a confirmation is issued.

Non-refundable reservation policy

Reservation fees compensate Steel City for holding professional time and preparing for the selected service. Once an appointment is booked or a live service window is reserved, the fee is non-refundable except when Steel City cancels or applicable law requires otherwise. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

Rescheduling, lateness and missed appointments

One reschedule is allowed when requested at least 24 hours before the appointment and is subject to availability. A late session still ends at its original end time. A missed appointment or cancellation with less than 24 hours’ notice forfeits the reservation fee, subject to applicable law.

Scope and additional charges

Each booking covers one primary issue or project need for the stated duration. Results are not guaranteed. Equipment, materials, permits, licensed trade work, manufacturer fees, additional labor and return visits are not included unless stated in writing. Additional work requires customer approval.

On-site travel zones

On-site fees are based on normal road mileage from central Birmingham to the service address. If a customer selects a lower-priced zone than the address requires, the difference must be paid before the visit is confirmed. Locations outside approximately 80 miles require a custom quote.

Remote support authorization

The customer must have authority to access the system and authorizes each remote connection. The customer is responsible for backups, credentials, internet service and an on-site helper when needed. Steel City may stop work that appears unsafe, unlawful, unauthorized or likely to damage equipment or data.

Installation projects and deposits

Full installation pricing and scheduling are governed by an approved proposal. An installation date is not reserved until the required proposal deposit is paid. For qualifying installation agreements of $1,000 or more signed within 30 days, Steel City may credit up to $99 of a consultation fee toward labor. Travel add-ons and completed troubleshooting time are not credited.

Customer responsibilities

The customer must provide accurate contact and site information, disclose known hazards, secure necessary property access and permissions, and ensure an authorized adult is available. Concealed conditions or inaccurate information may change the scope, price or schedule.
