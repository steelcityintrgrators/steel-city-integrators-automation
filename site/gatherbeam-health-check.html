How it works

One remote review. Clear next steps.

The pilot is intentionally simple so a church can get an expert outside perspective without scheduling an onsite visit first.

  1. 01

    Submit

    Share a recent service recording or livestream URL and describe the main problem.

  2. 02

    Document

    Provide the equipment and settings that matter: console, cameras, encoder/software, network and signal path.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    We review the evidence for likely audio, video, streaming, network and workflow causes.

  4. 04

    Prioritize

    You receive findings ranked by impact, urgency and whether the fix can be handled remotely.

  5. 05

    Improve

    Implement the fixes yourself, book remote optimization, or request onsite help only when physical work is justified.

What we review

The problem may not be where your team thinks it is.

We look at the full path from sanctuary source to online viewer instead of recommending equipment by default.

01

Audio

Gain structure, speech intelligibility, EQ, compression, routing, dedicated broadcast mix strategy and obvious signal-flow issues.

02

Video

Camera exposure, white balance, color consistency, framing, PTZ workflow, lighting interaction and switching presentation.

03

Stream & network

Resolution, frame rate, bitrate, encoder/software configuration, upstream capacity, reliability and common network bottlenecks.
