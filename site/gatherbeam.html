MINISTRYMESH ECOSYSTEM OFFERING

Meet MinistryMesh Reach.

MinistryMesh Reach brings church growth, outreach, visitor acquisition, Plan-a-Visit, follow-up, and connection together inside MinistryMesh. The historical ChurchReach AI work is being incorporated here as capabilities of the MinistryMesh ecosystem—not operated as a separate platform.

Church teams can prepare local outreach and visitor journeys in one connected workspace. Advertising integrations and AI-managed actions remain staged until the relevant church accounts, permissions, budgets, and approvals are configured.

MINISTRYMESH STREAM · DEVICES · APPS

One ecosystem across screens.

Existing MinistryMesh code includes Roku, Android TV/Google TV and mobile, Apple TV/tvOS and iPhone/iPad client shells, plus an Electron StreamDesk desktop shell intended for Windows packaging. Each connects through MinistryMesh Stream when an authorized service is configured.

These are prototypes, not released store apps. Production playback, signed installers, platform approvals, and physical-device validation are still required. Samsung Tizen and LG webOS are future work; client implementations were not found in the current code audit.
