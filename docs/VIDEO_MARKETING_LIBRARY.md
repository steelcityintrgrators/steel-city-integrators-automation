# Video Marketing Library and Production Plan

## Production standards

Format master:
- record vertical 4K or 1080p, 9:16 safe composition
- export 9:16 for Shorts/Reels, 1:1 for Meta feed and 16:9 for YouTube/landing pages
- add burned-in captions
- show real equipment, spaces, diagrams and hands-on work; do not imply a client relationship without permission
- use a strong first-frame headline and logo only after the hook
- keep awareness videos to one problem, educational videos to one lesson, and offer videos to one next step

Evidence rule:
- never invent customer names, results, savings, certifications or before/after claims
- until Steel City has an approved project case study, use “problem-to-plan” demonstrations labeled as examples
- obtain written permission before filming a client facility, screen, security layout or identifiable staff

Shared closing line:
“Steel City Integrators. Commercial systems designed, installed and supported from Birmingham.”

## Houses of Worship

Destination: /houses-of-worship.html

### 15–30 seconds — awareness/problem

Hook: “Your room can sound great while your livestream sounds completely different.”

Script:
“Your sanctuary mix is built for loudspeakers in the room. Your livestream needs its own balance. If speech disappears, music feels uneven or one volunteer carries the entire production, the workflow—not just the gear—needs attention. Steel City Integrators designs church sound and streaming systems around the room and the team using them.”

Shot list:
1. audio console meters
2. sanctuary loudspeakers
3. PTZ camera moving to a preset
4. headphones and stream monitor
5. simple split graphic: Room Mix / Stream Mix
6. landing-page CTA card

CTA: “See the church technology assessment.”
Usage:
- YouTube Shorts: in-market and custom-intent viewers around church sound/streaming
- Meta: local/regional pastors, worship/media interests and remarketing
- Google Display/YouTube: awareness creative leading to the worship landing page

### 30–60 seconds — educational/proof

Hook: “Three reasons church livestream audio fails—even with a good console.”

Script:
“First, the broadcast receives the same balance as the room, even though the room hears acoustic drums, voices and amplifiers directly. Second, there is no dedicated monitoring position, so the operator cannot hear what online viewers hear. Third, scenes and routing were never simplified for volunteers. The fix may include a separate broadcast bus, better monitoring, clear console scenes and training—not automatically a new console. We review the entire signal path before recommending equipment.”

Shot list:
1. presenter beside digital console
2. close-up of bus/routing page
3. room microphone and stage source
4. headphones/monitor speaker
5. volunteer-friendly scene buttons
6. annotated signal-flow overlay

CTA: “Request a remote consultation or on-site assessment.”
Usage:
- YouTube in-stream and organic education
- landing-page embedded explainer
- Meta retargeting for visitors who watched 50% of the awareness video

## 30-day production sequence

Week 1:
- film one awareness and one educational video for Houses of Worship
- film reusable B-roll: racks, cabling, digital console, PTZ, displays, camera, access reader
- create caption and end-card templates

Week 2:
- film Funeral Home and Trade Business awareness videos using privacy-safe demonstration locations
- edit all formats and create hook variants A/B

Week 3:
- film Daycare and Dental/Medical educational videos using diagrams and controlled demonstration footage
- avoid filming children, patients or sensitive data

Week 4:
- film General Commercial assessment explainer
- publish the first six awareness videos
- build remarketing audiences
- record baseline metrics in Campaign Performance

## Measurement

For each creative track:
- first-three-second hold
- 25%, 50% and completed views
- click-through rate
- landing-page request rate
- qualified-lead rate
- consultation/site-assessment rate
- proposal rate
- win rate
- revenue and gross-margin contribution
- cost per qualified opportunity

A high view count does not outweigh poor qualified-lead or revenue performance.
