# MinistryMesh.io Brand & Product Architecture

**Status:** Working ecosystem brand. The founder has authorized this public brand direction. This document is not trademark clearance, legal entity formation, or proof of domain control.

## Parent brand

**MinistryMesh.io** is the parent brand for the MinistryMesh church-technology ecosystem. Use **MinistryMesh** as the logo wordmark and **MinistryMesh.io** in parent-brand text, page titles, and brand disclosures.

The operating/legal entity currently evidenced in this repository is **Steel City Integrators LLC**. The brand update does not itself change company registration, contracts, accounts, domain registration, or tax status. Keep the operator disclosure on commercial pages until the founder completes any separate legal/entity steps.

## Positioning

**Ministry, media, and church technology—connected.**

MinistryMesh organizes practical church tools into one coherent ecosystem. Explain the real value in plain language and distinguish available services from beta, roadmap, and concept work.

## Internal operating systems

- **MinistryMesh LeadFlow** — MinistryMesh's institutional prospect and sales pipeline.
- **MinistryMesh CRM** — internal customer and service records. Separate from every church tenant's visitor/member CRM.
- **MinistryMesh Ops** — onboarding, support, usage, and internal operations.
- **MinistryMesh Edge** — historical concept; not customer-facing until its use case and technical role are verified.

## Naming rules

- Do not use GatherBeam as the customer-facing brand.
- Preserve old route names, campaign codes, database columns, asset paths, and repository identifiers only when changing them would break links, attribution, migrations, or deployment. Replace their visible labels and document the compatibility purpose.
- Keep Steel City Integrators LLC as the operator disclosure where applicable; do not imply it is legally renamed.
- Do not imply MinistryMesh.io domain ownership or trademark clearance based on the brand directive.
- Never show sample numbers, fake integrations, or conceptual device screens as production evidence.

## Visual direction

Preserve the current dark-navy, ice, electric-blue, and cyan website system, responsive structure, readable hierarchy, and interactions. Use a connected-node mark and restrained geometry. Maintain accessible contrast and avoid generic religious stock imagery or unsupported impact claims.

## Availability language

Only call the $149 MinistryMesh Remote health-check intake the current founding-pilot offer. Square checkout is not enabled in the current site flow; requests are qualified and confirmed manually. Mark other products as beta, roadmap, concept, or staged until the end-to-end product is tested.
