# Industry Funnel Architecture and Campaign Plan

## Shared operating funnel

Awareness video/ad → industry landing page → consultation or project request → CRM qualification → payment when applicable → scheduling → remote or site assessment → proposal → installation → follow-up → support retainer/review request

Payment is a workflow step, not the primary marketing message. Small support sessions may use hosted checkout before scheduling. Larger projects move from qualification to assessment and a custom proposal with the deposit or milestone terms stated in that agreement.

## Tracking standard

Every campaign link uses:
- utm_source: google, youtube, facebook, instagram, referral, email or organic
- utm_medium: cpc, video, social, display, email or referral
- utm_campaign: vertical + offer + quarter
- utm_content: hook or creative identifier
- campaign: internal landing-page campaign name

CRM qualification captures:
- organization and vertical
- location and approximate drive time
- problem/system
- scope and facility count
- timing
- budget band
- preferred contact
- campaign attribution
- next step: support, remote consultation, site assessment or project qualification

## Funnel 1 — Houses of Worship

Landing page: /houses-of-worship.html

Problem themes:
- speech and music coverage inconsistency
- livestream quality or operator complexity
- aging consoles, cameras, displays or cabling
- volunteer workflows that depend on one expert

Entry offers:
- remote system/workflow consultation
- on-site worship technology assessment
- custom sound, PTZ/streaming, lighting, LED wall or infrastructure proposal

Audience:
- pastors, executive pastors, worship leaders, media directors, trustees and facility leaders
- Birmingham-first; expand regionally for installation campaigns

Campaign plan:
1. YouTube/Google awareness: “Why the livestream mix sounds different from the room.”
2. Google Search: church sound system installation, PTZ camera installation for church, church livestream upgrade, digital console church Birmingham.
3. Meta remarketing: short before/after workflow videos and assessment offer.
4. CRM nurture: planning checklist, system-life-cycle email, assessment invitation.

Qualification emphasis:
- sanctuary size and seating
- current console/camera/streaming platform
- staff/volunteer operating model
- service schedule and installation windows
- budget and decision process

## Automation map

- request submitted: CRM create/update, score, Gmail acknowledgement, internal alert
- qualified: owner review and next-step assignment
- support reservation: provider verification, Calendar booking, confirmation
- appointment: 24-hour and 2-hour reminders
- assessment completed: proposal task and follow-up
- proposal issued: 2-day and 5-day follow-up; manual review at day 10
- project completed: review request, support-plan offer and recurring check-in
- suppression/opt-out: all marketing outreach stops regardless of score

## First campaigns to launch

1. Houses of Worship — Google Search + one 30–60 second “room mix vs stream mix” video. Strongest fit with existing expertise and portfolio potential.
2. Funeral Homes — narrowly targeted Search + direct business outreach around reliable service streaming. Clear consequence and specialized positioning.
3. HVAC/Plumbing/Trades — Meta awareness + Search focused on yard/vehicle CCTV and warehouse Wi-Fi. Broad local prospect base and practical entry projects.

Hold broader automated campaigns until landing-page conversion, qualification rate and sales capacity are measured.
