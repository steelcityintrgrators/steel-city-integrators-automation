# GatherBeam Brand System

## Brand idea
**GatherBeam** connects the sanctuary to everyone beyond it. The brand should feel like a global technology company first and a church-focused specialist second: precise, confident, modern, useful, trustworthy, and never gimmicky.

## Primary positioning
**Church technology, connected.**

## Hero line
**From sanctuary to everyone.**

## Product architecture
- GatherBeam Remote — diagnostics, troubleshooting and remote optimization
- GatherBeam Stream — streaming and broadcast platform
- BeamBox — managed church-side encoder / edge appliance
- GatherBeam Channel — web, mobile and connected-TV distribution
- GatherBeam Studio — service-to-content intelligence
- GatherBeam Reach — church growth, campaigns and visitor acquisition
- GatherBeam Visits — Plan Your Visit and follow-up
- GatherBeam Learn — training and certification
- GatherBeam Onsite — premium field services and AVL integration

## Visual system
- Midnight: #06111F
- Deep navy: #0B1F35
- Beam blue: #0F72FF
- Signal cyan: #55C7FF
- Ice: #F5F9FF
- Ink: #081321
- Muted blue-gray: #A9BED3

Use large white space, crisp dark sections, electric-blue accents, restrained gradients, high contrast and simple geometry. Avoid stained-glass clichés, crosses as default UI decoration, or generic church-stock imagery.

## Logo
The mark is a geometric beam / aperture form built from three overlapping planes. It should work as an app icon, device badge, streaming bug and favicon. The wordmark uses a bold modern sans-serif.

## Tone
- Clear, technically credible and pastor-friendly
- Explain outcomes before acronyms
- Avoid fear-based selling
- Never imply churches need new equipment when configuration or training can solve the issue
- Prefer "diagnose first" and "remote first"

## Founding-pilot disclosure
Until GatherBeam has its own finalized legal entity / infrastructure, public pages should say:
"GatherBeam is a founding-pilot brand operated by Steel City Integrators LLC while the remote-first church technology product is validated."

## Availability language
Only GatherBeam Remote / the $149 Health Check should be presented as currently available unless additional modules have actually been launched. Other modules should be labeled roadmap, validated next, or future capability.
