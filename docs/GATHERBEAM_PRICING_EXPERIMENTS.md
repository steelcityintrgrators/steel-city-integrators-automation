# GatherBeam Pricing Experiments — Founding Pilot

These are internal test hypotheses, not published final pricing.

## Objective
Price increasing levels of responsibility without underpricing technician labor, travel, security overhead, or support commitments.

## Tier 1 — Health Check
Current public founding pilot:
- $149 one-time

Primary purpose:
- diagnose;
- qualify remote readiness;
- identify Remote Technician or Onsite opportunity;
- capture structured technical evidence.

## Tier 2 — Remote Technician
Primary founding hypothesis:
- $399 scheduled remote optimization session
- target service window: up to 90 minutes of technician work
- additional time only with customer authorization

Alternative experiment if conversion is weak:
- $249 first 60 minutes
- additional technician time billed in defined increments

Do not publish both simultaneously. Test one offer at a time.

Included in pilot:
- Remote Ready verification
- defined scope
- pre-change snapshot where practical
- secure session
- authorized adjustments
- verification
- change log
- customer summary

Do not include:
- physical repair
- customer equipment replacement
- unsupported device access
- hazardous work
- unlimited support

## Tier 3 — Managed Support
Do not publish final subscription pricing until at least several paid remote sessions reveal labor demand.

Initial internal bands for testing:
- Essential: $199–$299/month
- Professional: $399–$599/month
- Priority: $799+/month
- Enterprise / multi-site: custom

Pricing variables:
- campuses
- rooms
- managed devices
- included support hours
- response commitment
- scheduled reviews
- technician session allowance
- stream monitoring
- documentation workload
- network complexity

Use a minimum gross-margin target rather than pricing from competitor anchoring alone.

## Tier 4 — Onsite
Quote:
Technician labor
+ travel labor
+ transportation
+ lodging
+ local transportation
+ parking/tolls
+ per diem
+ shipping/freight
+ special tools
+ additional technicians
+ after-hours/overtime
+ approved contingency.

Initial labor hypothesis for qualified technical field work:
- use Steel City Integrators' validated regional rate card when available;
- do not publish a nationwide hourly rate until technician-network economics are known.

## Travel cost rules
Local, regional and fly-in are operational categories, not fixed price zones.

For each quote:
1. resolve customer location;
2. identify an actually available qualified technician;
3. use the technician's real origin;
4. determine travel method;
5. calculate travel time and direct costs;
6. add onsite labor and required expenses;
7. disclose travel-related charges separately where practical.

Do not quote airfare, lodging, rental-car or per diem from estimates that cannot be reasonably confirmed.

## Margin rule
For every service calculate:
Revenue
- technician labor cost
- payment processing
- travel reimbursements not passed through
- software/access cost
- contractor cost
- refund/rework allowance
= contribution margin.

Do not scale a tier whose contribution margin is consistently weak.

## Validation metrics
- Health Check → Remote Technician conversion
- Remote Technician close rate
- average technician minutes
- revenue per technician hour
- rework rate
- Managed Support attach rate
- churn / cancellation reasons
- Onsite escalation rate
- travel recovery rate
- contribution margin
- founder time per customer

## Pricing decision rule
SELL → MEASURE → ADJUST.

A price becomes standard only after GatherBeam has enough paid customer data to understand demand, labor, support burden, and margin.
