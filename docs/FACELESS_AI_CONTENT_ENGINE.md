# Steel City Integrators — Faceless AI Content Engine v1

## Objective

Turn existing technical expertise into a repeatable, professional faceless media system that creates qualified demand for paid support, consultations, assessments and installation projects.

This file extends `docs/VIDEO_MARKETING_LIBRARY.md`; it does not replace it.

## Operating rule

Every asset must have one commercial job: attract, educate, qualify, convert, retain or upsell. View count is secondary to qualified lead and revenue contribution.

## Production chain

1. **Demand signal** — start from a real customer problem, search intent, objection, failure mode or buying trigger.
2. **One-sentence promise** — define the practical outcome before scripting.
3. **Evidence check** — verify technical claims and remove anything that implies an unverified client result.
4. **Spoken script** — short sentences, concrete language, no generic AI filler.
5. **Faceless visual plan** — equipment B-roll, diagrams, signal flow, screen capture, captions and original graphics.
6. **Voice master** — use one consistent, commercially licensed synthetic voice. Do not imitate a real person.
7. **Audio QC** — remove clipping, harsh sibilance, plosives, noise, unnatural pauses and pronunciation errors.
8. **Edit master** — create one high-quality source edit, then adapt to 9:16, 16:9 and feed formats.
9. **Conversion layer** — one CTA matched to one offer.
10. **Measurement** — track retention, click-through, qualified leads, consultations, proposals and revenue.

## Voice and audio specification

- Tone: calm, knowledgeable, practical, confident.
- Pace: approximately 145–165 spoken words per minute unless the topic benefits from slower delivery.
- Delivery: natural emphasis on the problem, technical insight and next step; avoid exaggerated “advertising voice.”
- Master when technically available: 48 kHz / 24-bit.
- Social/digital target: approximately -14 LUFS integrated and around -1 dBTP true peak unless the destination platform requires something different.
- Music: optional and subordinate to narration; never mask consonants or technical terminology.
- Pronunciation list: PTZ = “P-T-Z”; DSP = “D-S-P”; Dante = “DAN-tay”; NDI = “N-D-I”; Cat6 = “Cat six”; PoE = “P-O-E.”
- Reject the render if narration has obvious synthetic glitches, wrong stress, clipped words, mouth/noise artifacts or inconsistent loudness.
- Commercial-rights check is mandatory before publishing any synthetic voice.

## Visual specification

- Use real, permission-safe equipment footage when available.
- For facilities without filming permission, use diagrams, racks, close-ups, hands, screens with sensitive information removed, licensed stock, or original AI visuals.
- Burn accurate captions into short-form exports.
- First visual must communicate the problem within two seconds.
- Avoid long logo intros.
- Change visual state when the idea changes, not simply on a timer.
- Never expose credentials, security layouts, customer data, children, patients or private services.

## C05 — Network vs internet speed

# Repurposing map

For each approved master:
- 9:16 short
- 16:9 YouTube explainer when topic depth supports it
- 4:5 feed cut
- captioned still/carousel built from the 3–5 strongest teaching points
- 400–800 word SEO article
- FAQ entry
- email nurture snippet
- CTA link with UTM campaign/content identifiers

# Quality gate

Do not publish an asset until:
- factual accuracy >= 95/100
- clarity >= 90/100
- audio quality >= 92/100
- visual readability >= 90/100
- hook >= 85/100
- conversion alignment >= 90/100
- no unverified claims, fake proof, exposed credentials or privacy-sensitive footage
