# Data and Ownership Boundaries

## Stewardship
This repository is operated for **Steel City Integrators LLC**. **Media Tek Pros** is a DBA within the Steel City operating structure, not a separate repository owner.

## Separation rules
Do not place the following here:
- The Prepared Parent Project assets or participant data;
- ChurchReach AI, Church Live Network, or ClearSpeak AI proprietary source;
- AMW Family Trust, personal court, custody, or family records;
- client-owned source files without a written license or authorization.

## Allowed content
Website source, service descriptions, booking integration code, infrastructure documentation, sanitized demonstrations, marketing assets, and non-sensitive operating procedures.

## Third-party contributions
Before accepting material from a contractor or partner, document authorship, license, assignment rights, and any restrictions. Keep third-party notices and licenses with the applicable release.

## AI-assisted work
Record material human editing, architecture, selection, testing, and release approval. Do not assume an AI output is unique or free of third-party rights.
